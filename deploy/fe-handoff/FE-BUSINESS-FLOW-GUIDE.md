For every flow, use a new resource only through its authorized producer. Obtain IDs from prior responses or the owned scenario journal; never substitute fabricated database records. For 400/409/412/422 inspect the operation's exact structured error and refresh authoritative state before changing intent. Retain the original idempotency key and payload for an intended replay after an uncertain timeout. Do not retry every mutation automatically.

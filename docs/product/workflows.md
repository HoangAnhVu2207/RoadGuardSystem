## Source-linked transition inventory

Names in this table are **conceptual product states**, not code or wire enums. `H` means transferred historical content requiring module owner review; `C` means the cited PR requirement was confirmed on 2026-09-30. A confirmed direction does not approve an API, implementation, or unlisted guard. `Unknown` means the evidence or failure behavior must be agreed at the named module checkpoint. Full historical conditions and acceptance text are in [FR/BR](historical-fr-br.md), [US](historical-us.md), and [PF](historical-pf.md).
